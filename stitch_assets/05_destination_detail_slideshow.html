The Golden Coast of the North

Known affectionately as the "Boracay of the North," Patar Beach offers a dramatic stretch of coarse, golden sand that meets the vigorous waves of the West Philippine Sea. Unlike its highly commercialized counterparts, Patar retains a raw, unpretentious beauty that appeals to true explorers and nature enthusiasts.

The shoreline is punctuated by fascinating coral rock formations, providing not just scenic vistas but also teeming tide pools ready for gentle exploration during low tide. The coastal vegetation stands resilient against the sea breeze, offering dappled shade for those seeking a quiet afternoon with a book or a journal.
